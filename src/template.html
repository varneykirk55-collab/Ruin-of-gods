Controls

1 – 9Observe, Earthquake, Flood, Meteor, Divine Finger, Firestorm, Lightning, Tornado, Divine Deletion
Left mouseUse the chosen power. With Observe: drag to move, click a villager to follow
Right-dragRotate and tilt the camera
Shift-drag / middle-dragMove the camera while a power is selected
WheelZoom from a single villager to the whole valley
W A S D / arrowsMove · Q E rotate · R F tilt · + − zoom
[ ]Lower / raise power intensity
SpacePause · M mute · Esc stop following
TouchOne finger uses the power; two fingers move, pinch to zoom, twist to rotate

Earthquake: drag a line, release. Flood: drag a trench from the river toward low ground, or use it on the stone dam upstream. Tornado: click to touch down, then move the mouse to steer the funnel. Divine Deletion: the glowing ring is the brush. Click or drag to unmake the land under it, and anything standing there, into a sheer void. Water will run in. The dam and the outer edge of the map stay. [ ] widens the brush. Everything you break stays broken — Save keeps the scarred world, including what you deleted.
