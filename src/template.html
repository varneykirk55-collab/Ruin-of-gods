How to Play

Touch Controls

1-finger dragObserve / Move: pan camera · Powers: draw quake, steer tornado, pour flood, ignite or press
1-finger tapObserve: inspect & follow a villager · Powers: drop meteor, call lightning, or zap object
2-finger gesturePinch to zoom, drag to pan, twist to rotate (never triggers powers)
Move buttonDrag to pan while keeping your selected power. Tap Rotate to orbit and tilt with one finger. Tap the active camera button again to cast.

Mouse & Keyboard

1 – 8Observe, Earthquake, Flood, Meteor, Divine Finger, Firestorm, Lightning, Tornado
Left mouseUse power (with Observe: drag to move, click villager to follow)
Right-dragRotate and tilt camera · Shift/Middle-drag: pan anytime
WheelZoom from a single villager to the whole valley
W A S D / ArrowsMove · Q E rotate · R F tilt · + − zoom · [ ] intensity
Space · M · EscPause · Mute · Stop following / close modal

Earthquake: drag a fault line, release. Flood: tap or drag to pour water downhill without digging. Tornado: tap to touch down, drag or steer to guide the funnel. Everything broken stays broken — Save preserves the scarred valley.
